CINEVORA — PUBLISHING PACKAGE
==============================

1. Upload ALL files in this folder to a static hosting service.
2. Replace YOUR-DOMAIN-HERE in index.html, robots.txt and sitemap.xml with your real public domain.
3. Replace demo movie data, artwork and trailer links with content you own or are licensed to use.
4. Replace the placeholder Privacy and Terms pages with policies that accurately match your service.
5. After publishing, submit https://YOUR-DOMAIN-HERE/sitemap.xml to Google Search Console.

This package contains no backend, database, user accounts, payments or movie files.
